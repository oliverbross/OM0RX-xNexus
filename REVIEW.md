### Direct answers to the focus questions (TX safety)
- **After Halt / Stop TX:** stops. `halt_tx` → `on_halt` switches the pilot off at once (engine.rs:23179). Operator TX Off is seen on the next decode fold (autopilot.rs:376); until then nothing can re-arm, because `automation_switch` re-arms only if TX was on at entry. There is no race: all paths run under the engine lock and `self_tuning` is set only inside one synchronous call.
- **After a CAT or radio error:** see N-09; a refused QSY does not stop it. A radio handoff is overridden (N-01).
- **Outside the licence or band plan:** schedule moves use band-plan FT channels (safe). Hunts can go anywhere a spot says, and under the Open class nothing refuses (N-02, N-07).
- **Beyond 60/h, or after presence lapses:** possible through Answer callers (N-03).
- **TX watchdog:** not defeated. `arm = false` never resets it, and the watchdog start is restored after the re-arm (autopilot.rs:1145, 1167). Sequence advances reset it, as upstream does. See N-13.
